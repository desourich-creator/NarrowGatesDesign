Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

Most small business websites take a few weeks from our first conversation to launch. A one-page site can be quicker. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do I need to write all the content myself?

No. We'll ask you some simple questions about your business and help shape your answers into clear, friendly website text.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your domain name, your content, and your website belong to you.

Can I make changes later?

Absolutely. You can send us changes anytime, or choose a care plan so updates are included each month.

I already have a domain name. Can I keep it?

Yes. We'll connect your new website to the domain you already own.

Not sure what you need?

Tell us about your business and we'll recommend the right fit. No pressure.
