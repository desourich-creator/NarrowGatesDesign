Questions

AI, in plain English

Do I need to be good with technology?

Not at all. We handle the setup and explain everything in plain English. If you can send an email, you can use what we build.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. AI takes care of repetitive tasks like answering common questions and sending reminders, so you and your team can focus on customers and the work you do best.

Is AI expensive?

It doesn't have to be. Many AI tools cost less per month than a few hours of staff time, and the free audit shows you where the biggest wins are before you spend anything.

Is my business and customer information safe?

Yes. We use reputable, established tools, set them up carefully, and only connect what's needed. We'll walk you through exactly how your information is handled.

Can you add AI to my existing website?

Usually, yes. Chat assistants, automations, and AI search optimization can often be added to the website you already have.
