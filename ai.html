Questions

AI, in plain English

What is included in the free AI audit?

We review your website, your online presence, your marketing, and how your day-to-day runs. You get a short, plain-English report with the top ways AI could save you time or bring in more customers, ranked by impact.

Do I need to be good with technology?

Not at all. We handle the setup and explain everything in plain English. If you can send an email, you can use what we build.

Will AI replace my staff?

No. AI takes care of repetitive tasks like answering common questions and sending reminders, so you and your team can focus on customers and the work you do best.

Is AI expensive?

It doesn't have to be. Many AI tools cost less per month than a few hours of staff time, and the free audit shows you where the biggest wins are before you spend anything.

What is AI search optimization, and do I need it?

It's making sure tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity know about your business and recommend it. As more people ask AI for recommendations instead of searching, it is quickly becoming as important as traditional SEO.

Can you add these services to my existing website?

Usually, yes. Chat assistants, automations, care plans, accessibility fixes, and AI search optimization can all be added to the website you already have.

Is my business and customer information safe?

Yes. We use reputable, established tools, set them up carefully, and only connect what's needed. We'll walk you through exactly how your information is handled.

Do you work with other agencies?

Yes. We build websites and AI chat assistants under your brand for marketing agencies, SEO firms, and freelancers. See the Partner With Us section above.
