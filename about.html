About us

Faith and trust first. Clean design for the tech.

We're a small, independent web design studio that helps small businesses show up online with confidence.

Who we are

A designer in your corner.

Narrow Gates Design exists to give small businesses the kind of website they deserve: clean, fast, trustworthy, and built to bring in customers. No bloated agency process, no confusing tech talk, and no surprise bills.

When you work with us, you work directly with the person designing your site. We take time to learn how your business works and who your customers are, then build a website that points them clearly to the next step, whether that's calling, booking, or stopping by.

And we don't disappear after launch. We're here for updates, questions, and the small fixes that keep a website working for years.

Where our name comes from

Our mark is a cross that forms a narrow gate: its foot runs through the base of the tile, splitting it into two doors. It reminds us to put faith and integrity first, and to build websites with a clear, focused path for every visitor.

"Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it." Matthew 7:14 (KJV)
