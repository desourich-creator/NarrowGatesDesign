New here?

Plan your first visit

We know visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here's what to expect so you can feel at home from the moment you arrive.

  1. Arrive a few minutes early

    Friendly greeters will meet you at the front doors and help you find your way.

  2. Bring the kids

    Safe, fun classes for infants through 5th grade during both Sunday services.

  3. Grab a coffee

    Our welcome center has coffee, snacks, and people who'd love to meet you.

  4. Come as you are

    No dress code. Jeans are just as welcome as a suit.
